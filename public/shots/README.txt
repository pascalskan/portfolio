Screenshots are captured, not drawn. Method (chosen after comparing four on The Ledger):
  headless Chrome, deviceScaleFactor 3, PNG, clipped to the window that will be shown
  (1024x640 for the Ledger app, ~1240x775 section windows for the websites),
  then Lanczos-resized to 2400x1500 and encoded as WebP q92. Slides are 16:10.
Use invented demo data for The Ledger; get client permission for the two live sites.
