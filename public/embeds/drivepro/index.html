01 Beginner Lessons For complete beginners. Calm, structured sessions that build confidence from your very first time behind the wheel.
02 Nervous Driver Lessons For anxious learners. Patient, reassuring lessons designed entirely around your comfort and confidence.
03 Intensive Courses For learners who want to progress quickly. A concentrated schedule built around your availability and goals.
04 Refresher Lessons For licensed drivers returning after a break. Rebuild your confidence and update your habits at your own pace.
05 Motorway Lessons For new and returning drivers who want to feel safe at higher speeds and in faster-moving traffic.
06 Mock Tests For learners approaching their test. A realistic practice run to help you feel calm and well prepared.
07 Pass Plus For newly-qualified drivers. Official post-test training covering motorways, night driving and bad weather.

Do you teach manual or automatic?

All lessons are in a manual, dual-control car — a Nissan Qashqai — so you'll earn a full manual licence.

Where do you offer lessons?

Across Leicestershire and Northamptonshire, including Market Harborough, Kibworth, Oadby, Kettering and Corby, plus Rothwell, Loddington and the surrounding LE and NN areas. Message Maurizio to check he covers you.

How long is a lesson?

Standard lessons are 2 hours — long enough to settle in and make real progress each time.

What is your cancellation policy?

Lessons are paid in advance, and Maurizio asks for 48 hours' notice to cancel or rearrange.
